What happens when someone opens it

  1. 1

    The link opens this page

    It carries your repository's details, and nothing else. No account, no tracking, no request to any server of ours.

  2. 2

    The page hands it to Omnify

    Omnify opens on its add-a-repository screen with the address, the fingerprint and the username in place. Nothing is added until the reader says so.

  3. 3

    Or it offers to get Omnify first

    If nothing answers, the reader isn't left on a dead link: the page says Omnify isn't there yet and points at the download.

Why the link looks the way it does

Everything after the # in the link is kept by the browser and never sent to any web server, this one included. That is where your repository's details sit, so they travel from you to the person you sent them to and nowhere else.

Leave the password out

A password in a link is a password in someone's message history, on both phones, for good. Send the username in the link and the password another way; the person opening it has one field left to fill in rather than four.
